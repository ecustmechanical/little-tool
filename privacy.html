Privacy Policy

Last updated: 13 September 2026

AstroLens is a free, static website. It has no user accounts, no login, no checkout and no mailing list. This page explains the small amount of data that is collected, why, and what you can do about it.

The short version

  • Everything you type into a tool — your birth date, your name, your question — is processed inside your own browser. It is never uploaded, logged or stored by us.
  • The only data collected is anonymous, aggregated website analytics: which pages are viewed, roughly where visitors come from, and what device they use.
  • If advertising is shown on this site, the ad provider may set its own cookies. That is disclosed below and can be controlled from your browser.

Tool input

The numerology calculator, zodiac compatibility tool, zodiac sign finder and tarot reader all run as JavaScript in your browser. There is no server component and no database behind them, so your inputs cannot be transmitted or stored — this is a property of how the site is built, not merely a promise. Nothing you enter is retained, and closing or reloading the page clears it.

Analytics

This site uses Google Analytics 4 (property ID G-YWD0Z35ZZ9) to understand which pages are useful. It records anonymised, aggregated information such as the pages viewed, the approximate country or region, the referring site, and the browser and device type. It does not record your name, email address or the contents of any tool on this site.

Google Analytics sets first-party cookies to distinguish returning visits. You can block these cookies in your browser settings, in which case analytics will not run and the site will continue to work exactly as before. Google's own handling of this data is described in the Google Privacy Policy, and you can opt out of Google Analytics across all sites using the Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on.

Advertising

AstroLens may display advertising in the future to cover the cost of writing and hosting. If advertising is enabled, third-party vendors including Google may use cookies or similar technologies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this and other websites.

  • Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads based on your visits to this site and other sites on the internet.
  • You can opt out of personalised advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings, or opt out of third-party vendor cookies via aboutads.info.
  • If you are in the EEA or the UK, a consent notice will be shown before any advertising cookies are set.

Adverts on this site are clearly separated from editorial content. They never influence what a tool calculates or what a page says, and the calculations have no awareness of whether an advert is present.

Hosting and server logs

The site is served as static files by GitHub Pages. As with any web host, GitHub may record standard technical request data — such as IP address, user agent and request time — for security and operational purposes. AstroLens has no access to those logs as an analytics source and does not process them.

Children

AstroLens is not directed at children under 13, and no personal information is knowingly collected from them. The site requires no registration and collects no user-submitted personal data.

Your rights

Because this site holds no personal data about you, there is nothing for us to export or delete on request. If you are in a jurisdiction with data protection rights — such as the GDPR in the EEA or the UK, or the CCPA in California — the relevant processing is limited to the anonymous analytics described above, which you can refuse or block at any time through your browser or the opt-out links provided.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the date at the top of the page will be updated. Material changes — for example, enabling advertising or adding a new third-party service — will be reflected here before they take effect.

Contact

Questions about privacy on this site can be sent to contact@astrolens.com.
