Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 7, 2026

This page explains, in plain language, what information TackleCalc collects, why, and what you can do about it. Short version: our calculators run entirely in your browser, we don't sell your data, and we have nothing to hide.

1. The tools don't collect your fishing data

The Species Rig Builder and all current calculators run in your web browser. When you select a species or enter numbers, the calculation happens locally on your device. None of that input is sent to us or stored on our servers — it isn't personal, and we don't want it.

2. Cookies and analytics

This site may use a privacy-respecting analytics service to understand which pages are useful — for example, how many people visit the rig builder each week. Analytics cookies are optional, and you can block them in your browser settings without losing access to any tool on the site.

We do not currently use advertising cookies. If and when display advertising is added, this policy will be updated and advertising cookies will only be set with your consent as required by law (including under GDPR and CCPA).

3. Affiliate links

Some pages may contain affiliate links to tackle retailers. If you click one and make a purchase, the retailer may pay us a small commission at no extra cost to you. These links may set a cookie that lets the retailer credit the referral; that cookie is set by the retailer, not by TackleCalc, and we never receive any information that identifies you personally from it. Affiliate links are always clearly labeled.

4. Email and contact form

If you email us or use a future contact form, we keep the correspondence only as long as needed to answer it. We do not add you to any mailing list without your explicit permission, and we never share your email address with third parties.

5. Information we don't collect

We don't have user accounts, we don't require registration, and we don't ask for your name, location or any personal details to use the tools. If you don't contact us, we have no way to know who you are.

6. Third-party services

The site is hosted by a third-party hosting provider and may load fonts or other resources from third-party content networks. Those providers have their own privacy policies and process data (such as IP addresses) only to operate their services.

7. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct or delete personal data we hold about you. Since the only personal data we could hold is an email you voluntarily sent us, exercising that right is easy: email us and we'll do what you ask.

8. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes in a meaningful way — for example, when advertising is added — we'll update the date above and summarize the change at the top of this page.

Questions? Contact us.
