Pig (猪) — Chinese Zodiac Sign

Water element · Yin · Lunar year cycle: Jan 29 – Feb 15

What years are the Year of the Pig?

Pig years repeat every 12 years. Recent and upcoming ones: 1935 · 1947 · 1959 · 1971 · 1983 · 1995 · 2007 · 2019 · 2031 · 2043.

The Chinese zodiac year starts at Lunar New Year, not 1 January. If you were born in late January or February, check the exact lunar boundary — the year-only charts often assign the previous animal by mistake.

Pig personality and character

The Pig is the twelfth and final animal in the Chinese zodiac. People born in the Year of the Pig are known for their generosity, compassion and sincerity. The Pig symbolises wealth, honesty and a love of life in Chinese culture.

Pig frequently asked questions

What years are the Year of the Pig?

Recent Pig years include 1935, 1947, 1959, 1971, 1983, 1995, 2007, 2019, 2031 and 2043.

What element is the Pig?

The Pig is associated with the Water element. Five Pig types exist over the 60-year grand cycle.

What Chinese zodiac signs are most compatible with the Pig?

The Pig is most compatible with the Rabbit and Goat (its San He trine) and the Tiger (its Liu He secret friend). It clashes with the Snake.
