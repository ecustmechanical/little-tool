What years are the Year of the Rat?

Rat years repeat every 12 years. Recent and upcoming ones: 1924 · 1936 · 1948 · 1960 · 1972 · 1984 · 1996 · 2008 · 2020 · 2032.

The Chinese zodiac year starts at Lunar New Year, not 1 January. If you were born in late January or February, check the exact lunar boundary — the year-only charts often assign the previous animal by mistake.

Rat personality and character

The Rat is the first animal in the Chinese zodiac cycle. People born in the Year of the Rat are known for their quick wit, resourcefulness, and adaptability. According to legend, the Rat won first place by riding on the Ox's back and jumping off at the finish line.

Rat frequently asked questions

What years are the Year of the Rat?

Recent Rat years include 1924, 1936, 1948, 1960, 1972, 1984, 1996, 2008, 2020 and 2032. The Chinese zodiac follows a 12-year cycle, so Rat years repeat every 12 years.

What element is the Rat in the Chinese zodiac?

The Rat is associated with the Water element. Each element combines with the 12 animals in a 60-year grand cycle, so there are five types of Rats: Wood, Fire, Earth, Metal and Water.

What Chinese zodiac signs are most compatible with the Rat?

The Rat is most compatible with the Dragon and Monkey (its San He trine) and the Ox (its Liu He secret friend). It clashes with the Horse.
