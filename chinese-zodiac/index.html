How the Chinese zodiac works

Three layers stack on top of each other, and together they explain most of an animal's character.

  • Animal (地支) — twelve animals, one per year, repeating every 12 years. The animal sets the base temperament.
  • Element (五行) — Wood, Fire, Earth, Metal and Water cycle through the animals, producing five variants of each sign over 60 years. A Wood Horse and a Fire Horse are not the same person.
  • Polarity (阴阳) — alternating yang (active, outward) and yin (receptive, inward) gives each animal its direction of expression.

Animals also form fixed relationships with each other: San He (三合) triangles of three mutually supportive signs, Liu He (六合) pairs of secret friends, and Liu Chong (六冲) direct clashes. Every animal page shows its own, scored out of 100.

Chinese zodiac and Western zodiac together

The two systems answer different questions. The Western zodiac describes temperament through the season of your birth and the planets; the Chinese zodiac describes it through the year, the element and the hour. Reading both gives a rounder picture than either alone — start with the Western sign profiles, then add your animal here.
