What the three core numbers mean

Expression numberAll letters of the name. Your natural talents and the way you get things done — the widest of the three.
Soul Urge numberVowels only. What you privately want, the motive under the motive. Rarely visible to other people.
Personality numberConsonants only. The first impression you give, the outer shell before anyone knows you.

When the Expression and Soul Urge numbers agree, a person usually feels internally consistent and is easy to read. When they conflict, the gap between what someone wants and what they do becomes the central tension of their life — that is the point of reading all three together rather than one alone.

How the calculator works

Letters are converted with the Pythagorean table: A=1, B=2, C=3, D=4, E=5, F=6, G=7, H=8, I=9, then J=1 and the cycle repeats through to Z=8. Each group of letters is summed and reduced by adding the digits together until a single digit remains — except for 11, 22 and 33, which are treated as master numbers and left unreduced.

The five-element layer is a deliberate cross-system reading, not classical numerology. It maps the number onto one of the Chinese five elements so that it can be compared with your Chinese zodiac element and your Kua number.

Name numerology FAQ

What is name numerology?

Name numerology converts the letters of a name into numbers using the Pythagorean table (A=1 through I=9, then repeating). The totals produce three core numbers: the Expression number from all letters, the Soul Urge from the vowels, and the Personality from the consonants.

Which name should I use in the calculator?

Use the name you actually go by most often. Tradition says the birth certificate name shows your potential and the everyday name shows how that potential is expressed now. Try both and compare.

What are master numbers 11, 22 and 33?

In numerology, 11, 22 and 33 are not reduced further because they carry extra weight. They are read as intensified versions of 2, 4 and 6 — more pressure, more potential. The calculator keeps them unreduced when they appear.

How does this relate to the Chinese five elements?

It is a cross-system reading rather than a classical one. The calculator maps each numerology number onto one of the five elements — Wood, Fire, Earth, Metal, Water — so you can compare your name's numbers with your Chinese zodiac element and see where the two traditions agree.
