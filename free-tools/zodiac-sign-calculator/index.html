How the Sun sign is worked out

Your Sun sign is simply the section of the zodiac the Sun was passing through on the day you were born. The Sun spends roughly one month in each of the twelve signs, completing the circle in a year — which is why sign dates line up so closely with the calendar months.

This calculator uses the standard tropical date ranges for that. It does not need your birth time or place, because the Sun changes sign only once a month; the day is almost always enough.

The exception is the cusp. If you were born on or within a day of a boundary, the exact date the Sun moved can differ by a day from year to year, and the answer depends on the hour you were born. The tool flags those birthdays rather than pretending to certainty.

Frequently asked questions

What is my zodiac sign if I was born on a cusp?

If your birthday falls on or within a day of a sign boundary, you are on a cusp. The Sun changes sign on a slightly different date each year — usually by a day — so the safest answer is to check the exact time of your birth against an ephemeris. As a rule of thumb, the later part of the cusp day belongs to the following sign.

Is my Sun sign the same as my zodiac sign?

In everyday usage, yes. When someone asks for your zodiac sign they almost always mean your Sun sign — the sign the Sun was in on the day you were born. A full birth chart also includes your Moon sign, rising sign and the other planets, which is why two people with the same Sun sign can be quite different.

Why do the zodiac dates change from year to year?

The Sun does not cross each sign boundary at midnight on the same calendar date every year. Because the Earth's orbit is just under 365.25 days, boundaries drift by roughly a day across a four-year cycle. This is why cusp dates are approximate and a birthday near a boundary can go either way depending on the year.

What are the zodiac sign date ranges?

Aries runs approximately 21 March to 19 April, Taurus 20 April to 20 May, Gemini 21 May to 20 June, Cancer 21 June to 22 July, Leo 23 July to 22 August, Virgo 23 August to 22 September, Libra 23 September to 22 October, Scorpio 23 October to 21 November, Sagittarius 22 November to 21 December, Capricorn 22 December to 19 January, Aquarius 20 January to 18 February and Pisces 19 February to 20 March.
