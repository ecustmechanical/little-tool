How the daily fortune works

This tool uses a seeded pseudo-random number generator (mulberry32). Your zodiac sign index and today's date are combined into a single seed, so the same sign on the same day always produces the identical fortune — but a different sign or a different day will give a new result.

Fortune ratings range from 1 to 5 stars. Each reading includes guidance for love, career and health, plus a lucky color and number for the day. Refresh as many times as you like — the output will not change until tomorrow.
