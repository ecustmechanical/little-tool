How the five core numbers are calculated

This calculator follows the standard Pythagorean method, the most widely used system in Western numerology.

  • Life Path — the digits of your full birth date added together and reduced. Master numbers 11, 22 and 33 are kept unreduced.
  • Expression (Destiny) — every letter of your full name converted using A=1 … I=9, J=1 … R=9, S=1 … Z=8, then reduced.
  • Soul Urge — only the vowels of your name, converted and reduced. This is read as your inner motivation.
  • Personality — only the consonants, converted and reduced. This is read as the outer impression you give.
  • Birthday — the day of the month you were born, reduced to a single digit.

Where a name contains letters outside A–Z (accents, hyphens, spaces), they are ignored, which is standard practice.

Frequently asked questions

How is the Life Path number calculated?

Add every digit of your full birth date together, then reduce the total by adding its digits until you reach a single digit — unless you land on 11, 22 or 33, which are kept as master numbers. Example: 1990-07-14 becomes 1+9+9+0+0+7+1+4 = 31, then 3+1 = 4, so the Life Path is 4.

What is the difference between Life Path and Expression numbers?

The Life Path comes from your birth date and describes the lesson or direction of your life. The Expression (or Destiny) number comes from your full birth name and describes your natural talents and how you operate in the world. Most numerologists read the two together.

Should I use my birth name or my married name?

Traditional numerology uses the full name given at birth, because the Expression number is tied to your original name. Many practitioners also calculate the current name for comparison. This calculator lets you try either — the choice is noted in the method below the result.

What are master numbers 11, 22 and 33?

11, 22 and 33 are not reduced to a single digit in most traditions. They are considered intensified versions of 2, 4 and 6 respectively — more demanding, more capable and less comfortable to live with.

Does this calculator store my name or birth date?

No. All calculation happens in your browser with JavaScript. Nothing you type is transmitted, logged or saved anywhere.
