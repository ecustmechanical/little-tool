How the Kua number is calculated

The Kua number formula depends on your gender and whether you were born before or after the year 2000. Here are the four cases:

  • Male, born before 2000: (10 − digit sum) mod 9. If the result is 0, use 9.
  • Male, born 2000 or later: (9 − digit sum) mod 9. If the result is 0, use 9.
  • Female, born before 2000: (digit sum + 5) mod 9. If the result is 0, use 9.
  • Female, born 2000 or later: (digit sum + 6) mod 9. If the result is 0, use 9.

The digit sum is calculated by taking the last two digits of your birth year and summing them repeatedly until you get a single digit. For example, 1987 → 8 + 7 = 15 → 1 + 5 = 6.

Kua 5 is a special case: males use the Kua 2 directions and females use the Kua 8 directions.

Understanding East Group and West Group

In Feng Shui, people are divided into two groups based on their Kua number:

  • East Group — Kua 1, 3, 4 (and Kua 5 males). Lucky directions: Southeast, East, South, North.
  • West Group — Kua 2, 6, 7, 8 (and Kua 5 females). Lucky directions: Northeast, West, Northwest, Southwest.

Knowing your group helps you arrange your living space — your bed, desk, or front door should ideally face one of your four lucky directions.

Frequently asked questions

What is a Kua number in Feng Shui?

The Kua number is a personal Feng Shui number calculated from your birth year and gender. It determines your lucky and unlucky compass directions, helping you orient your home, office and bed for best energy flow.

How is the Kua number calculated?

Take the last two digits of your birth year, sum them to a single digit, then apply a gender- and century-specific formula. For males born before 2000 it is 10 minus that digit (mod 9). The exact formulas differ for males and females born before or after 2000.

What is the difference between East Group and West Group?

East Group people (Kua 1, 3, 4, and males with Kua 5) benefit from directions in the east sector — Southeast, East, South and North. West Group people (Kua 2, 6, 7, 8, and females with Kua 5) benefit from the west sector — Northeast, West, Northwest and Southwest.

What happens if my Kua number is 5?

Kua 5 is a special case with no assigned directions of its own. Males with Kua 5 use the Kua 2 (West Group) directions, while females with Kua 5 use the Kua 8 (West Group) directions.
