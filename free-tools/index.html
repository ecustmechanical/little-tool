What these tools do — and do not — claim

The numerology and compatibility tools are deterministic: the same input always produces the same output, and the page shows you how. The tarot reader is deliberately random, because that is how a draw is meant to work. None of it predicts the future.

Astrology and tarot are used here as reflective frameworks, not as science. Everything on AstroLens is offered for entertainment and self-reflection.
