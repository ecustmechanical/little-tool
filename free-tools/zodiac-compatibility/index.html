Zodiac Compatibility Calculator

Most compatibility charts just declare two signs "great together" and move on. This one shows its reasoning: pick two signs and you get a score built from three real astrological factors — element harmony, modality tension and polar attraction — plus a written explanation of what is helping and what is not.

Sun-sign compatibility only. Moon and rising signs matter too — see the note below.

Choose two signs and press Check compatibility to see the score and the reasoning behind it.

What the score is built from

  • Element harmony. Fire works with Air, Earth works with Water — each supplies what the other lacks. Same-element pairs are easy and comfortable. Cross pairs such as Air–Earth or Fire–Water score lower because they process the world at different speeds.
  • Modality tension. Cardinal, Fixed and Mutable signs approach everything differently. Two Cardinal signs both want to lead; two Fixed signs both refuse to move. Same-modality pairs take a small deduction, and the explanation calls it out.
  • Polar attraction. Each sign sits opposite a partner sign on the zodiac wheel — Aries opposite Libra, Taurus opposite Scorpio, and so on. These pairings are magnetic and complementary, so they earn a bonus.
  • Same-sign pairs. Two of the same sign understand each other instantly but provide no friction to force growth. They score well on ease, lower on challenge.

Scores are clamped between 35 and 96 on purpose. No Sun-sign pairing is a guaranteed failure or a guaranteed success, and presenting one as 100% would be dishonest.

Frequently asked questions

How is zodiac compatibility scored here?

The score combines three traditional factors: element harmony (Fire with Air and Earth with Water score highest), modality tension (two signs of the same modality compete, so a small deduction applies) and polar attraction (opposite signs such as Aries and Libra receive a bonus for complementary magnetism).

Which zodiac signs are most compatible?

In this scoring model the strongest matches are Fire–Air pairs (Aries–Gemini, Leo–Libra, Sagittarius–Aquarius) and Earth–Water pairs (Taurus–Cancer, Virgo–Scorpio, Capricorn–Pisces). Same-element pairs also score high for ease, though they share the same blind spots.

Does a low score mean a relationship will fail?

No. A Sun-sign comparison is the shallowest layer of compatibility. Real relationships also depend on Moon signs, rising signs, Venus and Mars placements, and above all on the two people involved. Treat the score as a conversation starter, not a verdict.

Can I check compatibility using birth dates instead of signs?

Yes — use the Zodiac Sign Finder to convert each birth date into a Sun sign first, then enter both signs here. Note that cusp birthdays can shift by a day depending on the year.
