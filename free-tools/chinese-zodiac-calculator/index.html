How the Chinese zodiac calculator works

The animal is derived from the year using the sexagenary cycle: subtract 4 from the birth year, take the remainder modulo 12, and read off the animal in fixed order from the Rat. The fixed element and polarity come from the same table, which is why each animal always carries the same element in this traditional scheme — the rotating five-element layer that produces Wood Horses and Fire Horses is a separate BaZi calculation.

Chinese zodiac calculator FAQ

How does this Chinese zodiac calculator work?

It converts your birth year into one of the twelve animals using the sexagenary cycle, then reports the fixed element, the yin or yang polarity and the compatibility score against any other animal you choose.

Does the calculator use the lunar new year boundary?

Yes. Because the Chinese zodiac year starts at Lunar New Year rather than 1 January, the calculator warns you when a January or February birth date sits close to the boundary, where a year-only chart would assign the wrong animal.

How is Chinese zodiac compatibility scored?

Scores follow the classical relationships: San He trine pairs score 95, Liu He secret friends 90, the same animal 75, same element 80, five-element feeding pairs 65, and Liu Chong clashes 20.
