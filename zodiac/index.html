How the zodiac is organised

Three attributes define every sign, and together they explain most of a sign's character.

  • Element — Fire (Aries, Leo, Sagittarius), Earth (Taurus, Virgo, Capricorn), Air (Gemini, Libra, Aquarius) and Water (Cancer, Scorpio, Pisces). Element describes temperament: Fire acts, Earth builds, Air thinks, Water feels.
  • Modality — Cardinal signs start things, Fixed signs sustain them, Mutable signs adapt. Each modality appears once per element.
  • Ruling planet — the planet that governs the sign's themes, from Mars (drive) for Aries to Neptune (imagination) for Pisces.

Signs also sit opposite a partner sign on the wheel — Aries opposite Libra, Taurus opposite Scorpio, and so on. These polar pairs are the zodiac's classic "opposites attract" combinations, and the compatibility calculator accounts for them in its scoring.
