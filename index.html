What makes TackleCalc different

Not another calculator farm — rig sheets with reasoning

The web is full of fishing calculators that ask for four numbers and hand back a tenth — and species articles that each contradict the next one. We built TackleCalc on the opposite principle: fewer, deeper, internally consistent answers.

  • One rig philosophy per species. Each fish gets one coherent rig sheet — rod, reel, line, leader, hooks, rigs, bait and tips — written so the pieces work as a system, not a parts list.
  • Reasoning, not recipes. When a choice looks odd — wire leader for pike, circle hooks for catfish, fluoro for walleye — the sheet tells you why, so you can adapt it to your own lake or river.
  • Written by an angler, checked by hand. The rig data is compiled and reviewed by the site's author, Marcus Reed, and updated as tackle and techniques change.
  • No paywalls, no sign-up walls. Free tools and free guides. If a product link appears, it's clearly marked as one that may earn a small commission at no cost to you.
