How this site works

Show the method, skip the mysticism

Astrology is a symbolic system, not a science — and pretending otherwise would be dishonest. What AstroLens does instead is take the tradition seriously and show its workings: which element, modality and ruling planet drive a sign, why a pairing scores what it scores, and how a numerology number is calculated.

  • Written, not scraped. Every sign profile and tarot interpretation on this site is written by hand and reviewed, not assembled from a template.
  • Calculators that show their maths. The numerology tool displays the exact digit sums it used; the compatibility tool lists the three factors behind the score.
  • Nothing leaves your browser. Every calculator runs locally in JavaScript. No birth dates, names or readings are transmitted or stored.
  • Free, with no sign-up. No account, no email capture, no paywall. If an ad or affiliate link appears it is clearly marked.

Quick answers

Frequently asked questions

How do I find out what my zodiac sign is?

Your Sun sign is determined by your birth date. Enter it in the Zodiac Sign Finder to get an instant answer plus your sign profile. People born on a cusp day (the first or last day of a sign) should confirm using their exact birth date and year, because the Sun crosses the boundary at a slightly different time annually.

Is this site's astrology actually calculated, or is it random?

Both, in different places. The numerology and compatibility tools are deterministic calculations with published methods. Tarot draws are deliberately random — that is how a tarot draw works. Daily readings are generated from your sign's element by the date, so they stay stable for the whole day and change the next.

Do you store my birth date or name?

No. Every tool runs entirely in your browser using JavaScript. Nothing you type is sent to a server, logged or saved.

What is the difference between a Sun sign, Moon sign and rising sign?

Your Sun sign is the one most people mean by "star sign" — it comes from your birth date. Your Moon sign reflects your emotional nature and your rising sign (ascendant) shapes how you appear to others; both require your exact birth time and place to calculate, which is why this site focuses on Sun signs and the tools that need only a date.

Is astrology scientifically proven?

No, and this site does not claim it is. Astrology is used here as a symbolic and reflective framework. Everything on AstroLens is offered for entertainment and self-reflection, not as medical, psychological, legal or financial advice.
