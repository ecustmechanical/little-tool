Species Guides

Fishing Rig Guides, Species by Species

Every species here has one complete, hand-compiled rig sheet: rod power and action, reel size, main line and leader, hook sizes, the terminal rigs worth tying and the mistakes worth avoiding. Each guide is written as a system — the rod, the line and the hook are chosen to work together, not copied from three conflicting articles.

Click any fish for its full guide, or open the interactive Species Rig Builder to compare rigs side by side and print a sheet for your next trip.
