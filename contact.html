Contact

Get in touch

I read every message. Email is the only channel — there is no form here, because a form would mean collecting your data, and this site is built not to.

Email: contact@astrolens.com

What I can help with

  • Corrections — a wrong date range, a ruling planet that is off, a maths error in a tool, or a claim that overstates what astrology can do. This is the most useful message you can send and I act on it first.
  • Topic requests — want a sign profile expanded, a birth-chart concept explained, or a tool that does not exist yet? Tell me what you were trying to do and I will consider it.
  • Tool problems — a calculator giving an answer that looks wrong, or a page that breaks on your device. Include the browser and the exact date or input you used, and I can reproduce it.
  • Partnerships, licensing and press — all welcome at the same address.

What I cannot do

I cannot give personal readings by email, predict an outcome, or advise on medical, legal, psychological or financial decisions. I cannot tell you whether to take a job, leave a relationship or invest in something, and I would not if asked. Those are yours to decide, and they belong with qualified professionals.

Before you write

For a correction, a link to the exact page and a sentence on what is wrong is all I need. For a tool problem, tell me the browser you are using and the exact input that produced the wrong answer — that is usually enough to find the cause quickly.

Replies are usually within a few days. Because the site has no mailing list and no newsletter, your address is used only to answer you.
