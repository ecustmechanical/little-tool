Contact

Get in touch with TackleCalc

I read every message. The fastest way to reach me is email — I try to answer within a couple of days.

Email: contact@tacklecalc.com

What I can help with

  • Species requests — want a rig sheet for a fish that isn't listed yet? Tell me your region and I'll research it.
  • Corrections — spot an outdated hook size, line class or regional rule on a rig sheet? Report it and I'll fix and update the page.
  • Rigging questions — a real question about adapting a sheet to your lake, river or bay. Include the species, water type and what gear you already own.
  • Partnerships & press — affiliate, licensing or media inquiries are welcome in the same inbox.

Before you write

A few things I can't do: I don't recommend specific retail stores, I don't build custom rigs for hire, and I can't give legal advice about fishing regulations — those belong to your state or provincial wildlife agency.

For the best answer, tell me: the species you're targeting, the type of water (lake, river, pond, bay), whether you fish from shore or a boat, and roughly what you already own. That's usually enough for a useful, specific reply.
