About AstroLens

AstroLens is a small, free astrology site: twelve written zodiac profiles, four browser-based tools, and no account, no email capture and no tracking beyond basic analytics.

Who writes this

My name is Elena Voss. I have been reading charts, tarot and numerology for a long time — first for friends, then seriously enough that I wanted a place to put it. AstroLens is that place. It is written and maintained by one person, which means fewer pages but far more care per page.

I am not a licensed therapist, doctor, lawyer or financial adviser, and nothing here is offered as any of those. Astrology, tarot and numerology are symbolic systems for reflection. They are interesting, useful and old — and they are not science, and I will not pretend otherwise.

How the content is made

  • Written, not scraped. Every zodiac profile on this site is written out by hand. The twelve signs are not mailbox copies of each other with the name swapped — the love, career, money and friendship sections each say something specific to that sign.
  • The method is shown. If a tool gives you a number or a score, the page also tells you how it got there. The numerology calculator shows the digit sums. The compatibility tool lists the three factors behind the score. You can check the arithmetic yourself.
  • Deterministic where it should be. The numerology and compatibility tools always return the same answer for the same input, because a calculation should. The tarot reader is deliberately random, because a draw is meant to be a draw.
  • Nothing runs on a server. Every tool on AstroLens is plain JavaScript running in your browser. Your birth date and your name never leave your device — not because of a promise, but because there is no server to send them to.

What this site will not do

It will not tell you your future, predict an outcome, or give you advice on a medical, legal, psychological or financial decision. If a reading here helps you think more clearly about something you were already deciding, it has done its job. If it starts making decisions for you, close the tab.

How the site is funded

AstroLens is free and intends to stay free. It may carry clearly-marked advertising, which is what pays for the writing and hosting. Adverts will never be disguised as content and they will never affect what a tool tells you — the calculators have no idea whether an ad is on the page.

Corrections

If something here is wrong — a date range, a ruling planet, a calculation, a claim that overstates itself — tell me and I will fix it. Corrections are the single most useful message you can send. The contact page is the place.
